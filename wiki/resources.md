# Resource library

> **Historic information:** These materials document the Turing's Research Community Management team's past practices. They remain open for RCMs and communities elsewhere to reuse, but review and adapt them before using them in an active project.

This library brings together reusable guides, reference material, and examples held elsewhere in this repository. The original files remain the canonical record.

## Project and community lifecycle

- [Research Community Manager offboarding checklist](https://github.com/alan-turing-institute/open-research-community-management/blob/main/resources/offboarding/offboarding-checklist-rcm.md) supports handovers when a community manager leaves a continuing project or when a community closes.
- [Sunsetting a project](https://github.com/alan-turing-institute/open-research-community-management/blob/main/resources/sunsetting/sunsetting-guide.md) outlines the team's draft approach to closing a project.
- The accompanying [sunsetting workflow](https://github.com/alan-turing-institute/open-research-community-management/blob/main/resources/sunsetting/RCM-sunsetting.drawio.pdf) and [documentation audit issue template](https://github.com/alan-turing-institute/open-research-community-management/blob/main/.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/SUNSETTING.md) support that process.

## Open research and recognition

- [Contributorship and authorship roles](https://github.com/alan-turing-institute/open-research-community-management/blob/main/resources/open-science-frameworks/authorship-roles-CRediT.md) adapts the CRediT taxonomy to describe community-management responsibilities across research stages.
- [Implementing Open Science: Strategies, Experiences and Models](https://github.com/alan-turing-institute/open-research-community-management/blob/main/resources/open-science-frameworks/ImplementingOpenScience-StrategiesExperiencesModels-ThematicReport-EC.pdf) is a reference report on open-science implementation.
- The [*The Turing Way* quick links](https://github.com/alan-turing-institute/open-research-community-management/blob/main/the-turing-way-core/quick-links.md) and [core-member roles](https://github.com/alan-turing-institute/open-research-community-management/blob/main/the-turing-way-core/roles-of-core-members.md) provide related community resources.
