## Section 2.3: Best practices that improve research quality

The paper links RCM work to Responsible Research and Innovation (anticipation, reflexivity, inclusion, and responsiveness) and describes five areas where RCMs champion good practice:

| Section | Area | Use it to... |
|---|---|---|
| 2.3.1 | Reproducibility | Support reproducible workflows across disciplines |
| 2.3.2 | Openness and transparency | Plan open practices and documentation |
| 2.3.3 | Accountability and innovation | Define shared responsibilities and review processes |
| 2.3.4 | Equity, diversity, inclusion, and accessibility (EDIA) | Build inclusive participation into project design |
| 2.3.5 | Fair attribution and recognition | Acknowledge all contributions; see the [contributorship roles guide](../resources.md#open-research-and-recognition) |
