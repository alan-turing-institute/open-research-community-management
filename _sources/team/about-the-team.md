# The historic team

> **Historic information:** This page records the former Research Community Management team's remit and approach. It does not describe the current structure of The Alan Turing Institute.

The TPS Research Programme hosted the RCMs within the **Open Research Community Management** team.
As community experts, RCMs were allocated to projects across different programmes and connected them as a joint-up system of open-source and reproducible software, data, people, and processes.
By sharing knowledge developed in different projects, both scientific outcomes and socio-technical approaches, the team worked to break down silos in the research environment.

RCMs combined socio-technical skills, including domain-research expertise, scientific communication, and stakeholder engagement.
Their responsibilities included ensuring access to the skills and resources that people and groups needed to participate meaningfully and build something greater than the sum of its parts.
Through collaboration, upskilling, and resource development, they made implicit knowledge explicit and enabled community members to move from spectators or users to developers and leaders.
The team facilitated open and collaborative work, embedded EDIA (Equity, Diversity, Inclusion, and Accessibility) practices, and developed shared frameworks, skill-building opportunities, and support systems for exchanging lessons from their work.
