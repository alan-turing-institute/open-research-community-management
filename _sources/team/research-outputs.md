# Research outputs

> **Historic information:** This page records research associated with the former Research Community Management team.

## Professionalising community management

The team published the following preprint on professionalising community-management roles in interdisciplinary research projects:

> Sharan, M., Karoune, E., Hellon, V., van Praag, C. G., Kayumbi, G., Bennett, A., Araujo Alvarez, A., Lee Steele, A., Batchelor, S., Lacey, A., & Whitaker, K. (2024). *Professionalising Community Management Roles in Interdisciplinary Research Projects*. arXiv:2409.00108. https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2409.00108

The preprint describes the role of Research Community Managers in connecting teams, initiatives, and resources to build resilient communities of practice in data science.

See the [guide to the paper](../paper-guide.md) for a section-by-section map of its practical uses.
