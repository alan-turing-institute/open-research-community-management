# Research Community Manager (RCM) Offboarding checklist

This is a list to be used by research community managers when they are off boarding from a project/community if it is continuing and also to be used if we are closing down a project/community.

## If project/community is continuing
### Logistics
- [ ] Write a handover document, with links to other documents/resources, to ensure understanding of where you are with your community work when you leave. 
    - [ ] Suggest and agree with line manager/project leads who can take over these tasks or if they will end.
    - [ ] Create a sharepoint folder to put all handover documents in and give access to project/programme manager and line manager.

#### Meetings
- [ ] Cancel calendar invites for meetings you hold and assign someone continuing in the team to resend them.
- [ ] Check use of zoom links that you are host for - cancel and assign someone to resend them. 

#### Community communications
- [ ] Email list/mailing list/project masterlist hand over or destroy (personal information)
- [ ] Newsletters - archive template/instructions of how to do newsletter and hand over task to team member or send last newsletter. 
- [ ] Webpages - ensure up to date and hand over task or record instructions on how to keep up to date. 
- [ ] Hand over or closure of Slack workspace/channels 
- [ ] Social media channels - hand over account to team member or close. 
    - [ ] Document methods used and archive any templates.

#### Project and community documentation
- [ ] Finish or handover tasks and report write ups you are responsible for (could be methods, approaches, or strategy)
- [ ] Update GitHub/Zenodo Archive
    * meeting notes
    * summaries
    * any other information that would help archival
- [ ] Archive any useful templates for community tasks
- [ ] Ensure project start up documentation is archived
    - [ ] Community background
    - [ ] Stakeholder mapping
    - [ ] Engagement plans
    - [ ] Community vision/roadmap 


### Pastoral
- [ ] Depending on the community: final event and/or thank you to community (card or could be done in email).
- [ ] Personal goodbye communications.
- [ ] Support offering for the community (where to find resources) or who is the new contact for community. 


## If project/community is closing down

#### Logistics
- [ ] Create an end of community document containing the community's current status and where resources and other documents can be found. 

#### Meetings
- [ ] Make community aware when meetings will end. 
- [ ] Cancel all meeting calendar invites and zoom links.

#### Community communications
- [ ] Archive email list/mailing list/project masterlist or destroy (personal information).
- [ ] Newsletters - archive template and instructions of how to do newsletter. 
    - [ ] Send last newsletter. 
- [ ] Webpages - ensure up to date and archive any instructions and templates . 
- [ ] Close Slack workspace/channels. 
- [ ] Close Social media channels and archive account details.  
    - [ ] Document methods used and archive any templates.

#### Project and community documentation
- [ ] Finish any report write ups you are responsible for (could be methods, approaches, or strategy).
- [ ] Finalise Sharepoint/Google drive/GitHub/Zenodo Archive
    - [ ] Change README to a close down message, add contact if there is one.
    * meeting notes
    * summaries
    * any other information that would help archival
- [ ] Archive any useful templates for community tasks
- [ ] Ensure project start up documentation is archived
    - [ ] Community background
    - [ ] Stakeholder mapping
    - [ ] Engagement plans
    - [ ] Community vision/roadmap 


### Pastoral
- [ ] Depending on the community: final event and/or thank you to community (card or could be done in email).
- [ ] Personal goodbye communications - email, meetings.
- [ ] Support offering for the community (where to find resources), links to relevant other communities, etc.
