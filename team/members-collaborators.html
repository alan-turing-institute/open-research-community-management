Members and collaborators#

Note: This is an archive of former team members, collaborators, and project affiliations at the Turing. It is not a current staff directory, and many of the people listed continue to work as RCMs or in related roles.

The Open Research Community Management team brought together TPS researchers who provided community-management expertise in Turing teams, projects, and programmes. It was established at the Turing in 2022 and operated until 2026. The team worked with other capabilities, including Research Application Management, Skills, and Research Engineering, and applied open-science and FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable) practices to community and research resources.

Resources and case studies from this work were shared through this repository and, where possible, through The Turing Way, with project-specific sensitive information removed.
